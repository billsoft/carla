如果还爆，按优先级逐步降低
优先级参数从 → 到节省显存1num_encoder_layers6 → 5~0.5GB2num_height_levels12 → 10~0.3GB3bev_size75 → 65~0.4GB4num_decoder_layers4 → 3~0.3GB
